WHAT IS FWI?

About the
Fire Weather Index

The Fire Weather Index (FWI) is a globally recognised numeric rating system that measures the potential intensity of a wildfire based on weather and fuel moisture conditions. Developed by the Canadian Forest Service, it integrates multiple environmental factors to compute a single risk number.

FWI Tempest uses a trained Ridge Regression model with StandardScaler normalisation to predict the FWI score for any given set of meteorological inputs — giving forest officers, safety teams, and researchers an instant, data-driven fire risk assessment.

Fuel Moisture

FFMC, DMC and DC indices track moisture in fine fuels, duff layers and deep organic matter.

💨

Weather Dynamics

Temperature, relative humidity, wind speed and rainfall all feed into the computation.

📈

Behaviour Indices

ISI captures fire spread rate while BUI reflects fuel availability — together they define FWI.

APPLICATIONS

Key Roles of
FWI Tempest

01

Forest Fire Prevention

Equip forest rangers and fire departments with daily FWI scores to plan preventive measures before conditions turn critical.

02

Community Safety Alerts

Issue timely advisories to rural and forest-edge communities about elevated fire risk, enabling early evacuation readiness.

03

Agricultural Protection

Help farmers and landowners safeguard crop fields by understanding when dry and windy conditions pose a serious burn risk.

04

Climate Research

Support environmental scientists in analysing fire-weather patterns and correlating FWI trends with climate change indicators.

05

Emergency Response

Aid incident commanders in resource allocation — positioning aerial water bombers and ground crews where risk is highest.

06

Policy & Governance

Provide government bodies and disaster management authorities with model-backed data for policy-making and risk zoning.
